Anchor is a recovery app for people trying to stay sober from nicotine, alcohol, or drugs. It acts as a de-addiction agent, preventing overdoses and reducing the risk of overdose-related fatalities. Users track their sober day counts, report their well-being log, and access calming exercises. Users can also log their cravings and triggers. Over time Anchor tracks which ones affect them the most so they can spot patterns from their own data. It also supports an always on de-addiction coach, Tide. Tide is an AI coach that runs entirely on their phone to ensure no data goes beyond the device. Tide asks about their reasons for recovery and helps them make a plan, using the method of motivational interviewing rather than lecturing. Anchor connects to Apple Watch to watch for signs of an overdose or alcohol emergency by using metric co-relations built through a fine tuned AI model based on past overdose data from CDC. It looks for different warning signs depending on the de-addiction. For opioids, it looks for slowed breathing, low blood oxygen, falling heart rate as the main signals. For alcohol, it looks for a dangerously low or high heart rate, and for nicotine, it looks for a dangerously high heart rate. The on-device learning system keeps improving to understand the limits of high and low for the specific user over time. If the risk becomes critical and the person doesn't respond, it sounds an alarm for anyone nearby, brings up a 911 call, and sends a text with their location to their emergency contacts. Those contacts are entered during the onboarding process. The goal being to prevent loss of life due to overdose.
